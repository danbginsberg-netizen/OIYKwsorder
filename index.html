About

About Only If You Know

Only If You Know is a New York jewelry house — color-led, handmade design with a clear point of view a buyer can build a story around. Every piece is designed by Kate Choi, whose work has supplied specialty boutiques and the American wholesale market for nearly two decades and has been featured at New York Fashion Week, including a collaboration with the label LIE.

The line is handmade in New York from seed beads, gemstones, freshwater pearls, mother-of-pearl, shells and ceramics, and is shown at Coterie New York and MAGIC Las Vegas. Most styles are made to order, with 45- to 60-day lead times depending on the season; reorders ship from our U.S. warehouse in about 10 business days.
